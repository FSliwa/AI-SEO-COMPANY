How to use this template

  1. Start with the business details sheet (page 2): one version of your name, address and phone number is the master for every other check.
  2. Work through areas A and B before you create the profile. Don’t change the name, address or category during verification.
  3. Mark a status for every check: OK, to fix, or N/A (not applicable).
  4. Describe the problem in the notes: what’s wrong, who fixes it and by when, e.g. “old number in 2 directories, reception, by Friday.”
  5. High priority first, then medium and low. Check NAP consistency in the table on the last page.
  6. Fill in the log every month, and go through the whole list again every quarter and after any change in the business.

Statuses and priorities

  • OK – check passed
  • Fix – to fix: note the problem, owner and deadline
  • N/A – doesn’t apply to your business; leave it out of the score
  • high blocks setup, verification or visibility in Maps; do it first
  • medium limits results, but the profile works
  • low fine-tuning; do it alongside other changes
