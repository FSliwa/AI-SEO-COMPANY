How to use this template

  1. Record a baseline: non-brand clicks and customer inquiries (table at the bottom of this page).
  2. Collect access: Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile, CMS, robots.txt. Missing access is your first finding.
  3. Work through areas A–E in order and mark each check OK, needs work or N/A.
  4. Describe the problem in the notes: the URL and the specific issue, e.g. “/contact: no H1.”
  5. Set the order: high priority first. Give every open check an owner and a deadline.
  6. Recheck after the fixes and fill in the summary. Repeat the full list every quarter.

Statuses and priorities

  • OK – the check passes
  • Fix – needs work: note the URL and the problem
  • N/A – doesn’t apply to your business; leave it out of the score
  • high blocks visibility in Google or Maps, or blocks measurement; do it first
  • medium holds results back, but the site works and is visible
  • low polishes the details; do it alongside other changes
