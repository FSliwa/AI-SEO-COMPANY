SEO competitor analysis template

A worksheet for your business and up to five competitors. You compare seven areas: Google rankings, Google Maps, AI answers, on-page content, technical basics, backlinks, and offer and pricing. Score each one from 0 to 2, then turn the gaps into an action list.

How to use this template

  1. Cover sheet. Write down your business, market, date, and the location and device you check from. Add 10 keywords from Search Console, Google autocomplete and customer questions.
  2. Competitors. Take the 3 domains seen most often in the top 10, the Google Maps leader and a company that ChatGPT or Perplexity recommends.
  3. Google and Maps. In a private window, check every keyword (table 1) and the 3-pack for 3 keywords with your city name (table 2).
  4. AI answers. Ask 5 customer questions in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google. Record the companies named and the cited pages (table 3).
  5. Competitor websites. Fill in tables 4–7. Write the tool and date next to every number.
  6. Scores. Give 0–2 points using the scoring guide on page 5 and add them up (maximum 14 points per company).
  7. Actions. Put every gap where a competitor leads into part 8 with a priority, an owner and a deadline.
