Keyword research template: from a seed list to a keyword map

Eight parts: seed keywords from four sources, keyword scoring, a SERP overlap test, a keyword map, ICE priorities and a monthly cannibalization check. Fill it in by hand or in a PDF editor. Rows in italics are an example for a fictional accounting firm.

How to use this template

  1. Describe the business and the goal (part 1): services, service area, language and what you do not sell.
  2. Collect seed keywords from four sources (part 2). Just collect; do not judge yet.
  3. Add search volume, intent and current position (part 3). No number available: write “no data”.
  4. Run the overlap test for pairs of keywords (part 4) and group them into clusters.
  5. Give each cluster one target page and an action (part 5).
  6. Set the order with ICE scores (part 6).
  7. Check for cannibalization every month (part 7). Before you close the map, go through the checklist in part 8.

Intent codes

  • I informational: a guide, definition or how-to
  • C commercial: comparisons, prices, reviews before deciding
  • T transactional: a specific offer, contact or purchase
  • L local: a business nearby, map pack in the results
  • N navigational: a specific business or website

Template rules

  • Overlap test: 4+ shared URLs out of 10 → one cluster; 2–3 → compare page types in the top 5 and repeat on another day; 0–1 → separate clusters.
  • One cluster = one page. Two pages chasing the same keywords weaken each other.
  • ICE: impact on leads × confidence × ease, each scored 1–10.
  • Quick wins: keywords in positions 4–20 in Search Console.

2. Seed keywords from four sources

Write down everything that fits your offer. You score keywords in part 3. Each source sees a different slice of demand, so use all four.

Remember: “no data” in Keyword Planner does not mean nobody searches. Keyword Planner hides very low-volume keywords. If a keyword keeps coming up in customer questions and autocomplete, keep it. Google Trends shows relative values on a 0–100 scale, not search counts.

8. Keyword research checklist before you close the map

The map is ready when every item is ticked.

  • Every keyword has a source for its search volume, or is marked “no data”
  • The intent of every cluster has been checked in Google’s results
  • Every cluster has exactly one target page
  • No page is assigned to two clusters with the same intent
  • Question keywords have a planned direct answer on the page (first paragraph under a question heading)
  • High-priority clusters are in the SEO strategy and the content calendar
