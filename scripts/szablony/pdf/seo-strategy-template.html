SEO strategy template: your 12-month SEO plan

Your SEO strategy on one document: from the business goal through keywords, target pages, content, technical SEO, local SEO, links and AI search visibility, to a quarterly roadmap, measurement, risks and budget. Fill it in by hand or in a PDF editor.

How to use this template

  1. Capture your baseline (part 3): 12 months of Search Console and GA4 data, your Google Business Profile, and AI answers to customer questions. Write down the date.
  2. Turn the business goal into an SEO goal (part 2): a number of organic leads, a deadline and a data source.
  3. Group keywords into clusters (part 4): one cluster, one target page.
  4. Pick technical, local and link priorities (parts 6–8): only what blocks the goal.
  5. Plan content and build the quarterly roadmap (parts 5 and 10): one objective and one success measure per quarter.
  6. Set measurement, risks, owners and budget (parts 9, 11 and 12).
  7. Fill in part 1 last, the one-page summary, and book the first quarterly review.
