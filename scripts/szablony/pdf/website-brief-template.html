Website brief template

Fill it in before you ask for quotes: 12 sections, a sitemap, an SEO section with redirects, budget and timeline.

How to use this template

  1. Complete the brief before you request quotes. One person owns the document and collects answers from sales and customer support.
  2. Be specific: what should happen, for whom, and how you will measure it. Skip adjectives like “modern” or “professional.”
  3. If you don’t know an answer yet, write “to be decided.” A brief with a few gaps sent today beats a perfect one sent next month.
  4. Fill in section 8 (SEO) with the person who has access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4.
  5. Add the attachments listed in section 12 and send the same brief to every vendor. Ask for proposals in the same format.

SEO checklist before moving the site

  • Export of top pages and queries from Search Console (12 months)
  • List of pages that generate leads (GA4)
  • Full list of current URLs (sitemap, CMS, Search Console)
  • 301 redirect map: old URL → closest equivalent
  • Redirects kept as long as possible; Google says generally at least a year
  • Internal links updated to the new URLs
  • New sitemap submitted in Search Console
  • Titles and descriptions of key pages carried over or improved
  • Business name, address and phone match the Google Business Profile
  • Analytics and key events work from launch day
