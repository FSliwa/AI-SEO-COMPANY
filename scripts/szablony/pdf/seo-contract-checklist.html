Part 2. Model B2B Clause Framework (Educational Reference)

Clauses 1-2. Countable Scope & 1-Month Email Notice
Provider delivers itemized monthly outputs in Schedule A (technical fixes, content count, editorial links, Business Profile). Agreement runs on a monthly rolling basis terminable via email with 1 calendar month’s notice and zero exit penalties or silent 12-month auto-renewals.
Clause 3. First-Party Account Ownership
Client remains sole Primary Owner of Google Search Console, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Business Profile, Bing Webmaster Tools and ad accounts under Client’s own domain email. Provider operates strictly via delegated user/manager access.
Clause 4. Permanent Content & Code IP
Upon payment of each monthly invoice, full economic copyright (and an irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide royalty-free license with modification rights) in all copy, graphics, metadata and code vests in Client. Provider may never delete paid work post-termination.
Clause 5. Permanent Backlinks & Anti-Spam Rule
Provider complies with Google Search Essentials and spam policies (zero PBNs, link schemes or cloaking). Earned placements from paid billing cycles are never removed by Provider after contract end, and exact live URLs are reported monthly.
Clauses 6-8. Approval, Fixed Net Fee & GSC/GA4 Reports
Provider logs all changes and obtains email sign-off before publishing pages or template edits. Fixed monthly net fee with zero hidden offboarding charges. Monthly report pulled from Client’s own GSC and GA4 (non-brand clicks, key events, work log, live link URLs).
Clauses 9-12. Standard of Care, NDA & GDPR Art. 28
Professional services standard of care without deceptive #1 ranking guarantees. Mutual confidentiality of business data. Balanced liability without termination penalties. Attached GDPR Art. 28 DPA whenever CMS/CRM personal data is accessed; access revoked within 7 business days of exit.
