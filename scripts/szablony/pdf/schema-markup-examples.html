How to use this template

  1. Collect your business details in part 1: name, address and phone exactly as on your Google Business Profile.
  2. Assign types to pages in part 2 and write down the URLs where each type will go.
  3. Copy a template from part 3 and replace the highlighted values. Delete fields that don’t apply, together with their commas.
  4. Add the code in a <script type="application/ld+json"> tag in the head or body, or enter the details in your SEO plugin.
  5. Test it in the Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator, then work through the checklist in part 4.
  6. Log the result in part 5 and check the page with URL Inspection in Search Console.

Part 4. Pre-publication checklist

  • Rich Results Test shows no critical errors
  • Schema Markup Validator (validator.schema.org) shows no syntax errors
  • Every value in the code is visible on the page: name, address, hours, price, author
  • Name, address and phone identical in the code, on the page and on your Google Business Profile
  • Required properties filled in, no placeholder values left (example.com, 62701)
  • The most specific matching type, such as Dentist rather than LocalBusiness
  • No star ratings about yourself in LocalBusiness or Organization
  • Logo and images crawlable by Googlebot, not blocked in robots.txt
  • One block per business: theme and plugin don’t duplicate it
  • Page has no noindex, isn’t blocked in robots.txt and isn’t behind a login
  • After publishing: URL Inspection in Search Console
  • Review date in the calendar: after any change to business details, prices or templates
