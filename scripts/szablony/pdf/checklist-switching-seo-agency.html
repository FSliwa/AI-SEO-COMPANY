Before you give notice: 5 things to check in the contract

Take the agency’s contract and terms of service. These five points decide how much you will pay and what stays with you.

  1. Notice period. How long it is and when it starts running: from the day the notice is delivered or from the end of the month. One day of delay can extend the contract by a whole period.
  2. Automatic renewal. Whether a fixed-term contract renews on its own, and by what date you must give notice to prevent that.
  3. Exit fees. Contractual penalties, an early termination fee, a clawback of the discount for a long commitment. Work out the cost before you decide.
  4. Content rights. Whether the texts, photos and graphics pass to you and, if so, from when (often only after the last invoice is paid).
  5. Links after the contract ends. Whether the links are yours or rented. Links from the agency’s private network usually disappear with the contract, and with them part of your rankings.
